# v1.1.1b patch

This patch implements your hypercritical fixes:
- **"Log Night" model** indexed by bedtime date, so dose 2 and final wake after midnight stay on the same record
- **Ordering validation** with auto-normalization across midnight and user alerts on impossible sequences
- **Clinician-friendly CSV** that shows HH:mm times with the night date in the first column
- **Grams auto-populated** from the latest suggestion, rounded to **0.25 g** increments
- **WHOOP auto-fill** button for final wake (pulls `/api/sleep/latest` from your proxy)
- **Dose 2 reminder** scheduled automatically when you stamp Dose 1 and a window start is known
- **First-run onboarding** to collect bedtime and total grams before logging
- **AppIntents shell** to enable one-tap logging from a widget (wire to App Group for full background writes)
- **Proxy pagination + aggregates** code snippet to support 7 day averages and respect WHOOP's page size

## Files
- ios/Models.swift
- ios/LogButtonsView.swift
- ios/TodayLogView.swift
- ios/AppIntents.swift
- server/index.additions.js (paste into your Node proxy as indicated)

## Wiring guide
1. Add/replace the Swift files in your Xcode project, ensure SwiftData is enabled and model container includes `DoseLog`.
2. In your main view, pass the nightly suggestion grams and window start into `TodayLogView` so it can pre-fill and schedule reminders.
3. In the proxy, paste `server/index.additions.js` parts into your `index.js`:
   - add `express-rate-limit`,
   - swap range fetches to use `getJsonWithPagination`,
   - add `/api/aggregates/7days`.
4. Confirm your proxy `.env` has `API_KEY` and lock CORS to localhost during dev.

## Notes
- HealthKit auto-fill for wake is the next enhancement; this patch includes WHOOP auto-fill as a starting point.
- All suggestions remain planning aids, not medical advice. Keep total grams and per-dose bounds aligned to your clinician's plan.
